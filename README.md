# Data Visualization and Storytelling – Superstore

## Internship
Elevate Labs – Data Analyst Internship

## Task
Task 2: Data Visualization and Storytelling

## Tool Used
Microsoft Power BI

## Dataset
Superstore.csv

## Objective
The objective of this task was to create visualizations that communicate sales performance clearly and present meaningful business insights through data storytelling.

## Visualizations Created
- Total Sales KPI
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Segment

## Key Business Insights
- The East region has the highest sales among the four regions.
- Office Supplies generates the highest sales among the categories.
- The Consumer segment contributes the largest share of sales.

## Data Storytelling
The dashboard presents the overall sales performance first and then breaks it down by region, category, and customer segment. The key business insights highlight the major patterns visible in the data and make the dashboard easier to understand for decision-making.

## Dashboard
The Power BI dashboard screenshot is included in this repository as the visual deliverable for the task.

## Conclusion
The dashboard transforms the Superstore sales data into simple and easy-to-understand visualizations. It helps identify the strongest region, leading category, and largest customer segment while presenting the findings in a clear visual format.
